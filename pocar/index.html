pocăr

July 2026 · solo project

Private Texas Hold'em for a group of friends: live nine-seat tables over Socket.io, built-in bots and an API for your own, profiles and statistics. For real-money games the host collects buy-ins outside the app, and pocăr records who sent, who confirmed and who was paid.

The hard part

Getting the money right. Side pots are paid in layers by how much each player put in, and every action is validated on the server. A test plays 300 random hands and checks that no chip is ever created or lost. Writing it turned up a raise that could create chips and a side-pot payout bug, and both are fixed.

Built with

Next.js, React, TypeScript, Socket.io, PostgreSQL, Prisma, NextAuth.
