ClearPath

March 2026 · First place, Calvin University Hackathon · project lead

A driving assistant for colorblind, elderly and low-vision drivers. A phone mounted on the dashboard watches the road, a computer-vision backend finds traffic lights, stop signs and pedestrians, and the app announces them with high-contrast visuals and recorded voice prompts in four languages. It won first place at the Calvin University Hackathon in March 2026.

The hard part

Color at a distance. A far-away traffic light is only a few pixels wide, and reading its color reliably is still the main open problem.

Built with

Expo, React Native, FastAPI, YOLOv8.
