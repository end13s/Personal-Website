AI Grading Tool

February 2026 · Calvin University

Grades intro-CS Python labs. Import the Moodle roster and a ZIP of submissions, and an LLM answers yes, no or unsure for every rubric item. The instructor reviews those answers and exports grades and feedback back to Moodle. It can also suggest new rubric items for mistakes that keep coming up, check an exercise's requirements, and run turtle-graphics programs in the browser.

The hard part

Keeping the instructor in charge. The model never assigns a grade. It answers narrow questions about rubric items the instructor wrote, and in batch mode only clear yes answers are applied on their own.

My part

It builds on the Moodle Grading App, made by earlier Calvin students with Victor Norman as advisor. I turned it into an AI grader: submission import and matching, the code viewer, every AI workflow, the ZyBooks lab flow and the bring-your-own-key setup. Full credits are on GitHub.

Built with

React, TypeScript, Vite, OpenAI, Pyodide.
